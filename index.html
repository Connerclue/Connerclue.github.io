About

A practical start in cybersecurity.

I’m pursuing a B.S. in Computer Science with a cybersecurity focus. My interest started with learning how computers and networks fit together, then grew through school district IT work and four seasons of CyberPatriot competition.

I like troubleshooting from the evidence, learning how a system is supposed to behave, and explaining a fix in a way that makes sense to the person who needs it. I’m especially interested in defensive security, endpoint systems, networking, and practical automation.

As a first-year student, I’m still growing my public project portfolio. I’d rather share a small piece of work with clear context and honest limits than overstate what it demonstrates. This site will develop as I complete coursework and personal labs.
